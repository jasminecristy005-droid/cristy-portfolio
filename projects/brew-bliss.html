02 / DIGITAL EXPERIENCE CASE STUDY

Brew & Bliss From visitor to customer.

A coffee brand landing page designed to turn a simple online visit into a clear, guided customer journey.

I built the experience from the visual landing page through lead capture, ordering, database storage, and administrative order tracking.

COFFEE / DIGITAL EXPERIENCE

Brew & Bliss

THE PROJECT

A landing page with a purpose.

Brew & Bliss was created as a complete digital funnel rather than a static website. The experience connects brand presentation, lead capture, ordering, and operational tracking into one flow.

ROLE Design & Development
STACK HTML / CSS / JavaScript
INTEGRATION Formspree / Supabase
WHAT I BUILT
01

Brand Landing Page

A responsive coffee brand experience designed around clear messaging, visual hierarchy, and strong calls to action.

02

Lead Capture

A dedicated form allows visitors to submit their information and enter the brand's customer journey.

03

Online Ordering

Customers can select products, quantities, order type, and additional instructions through the ordering experience.

04

Order Database

Order records are stored in Supabase so information can persist beyond the browser session.

05

Admin Order Tracking

An administrative dashboard allows orders to be monitored and moved through operational statuses.

06

Customer Journey

The project connects discovery, lead capture, ordering, confirmation, and order tracking into one continuous experience.

THE VALUE

Not just a pretty page.

The goal was to demonstrate how a small business can connect its customer-facing website with the operational systems behind it. Instead of treating the website, forms, orders, and administration as separate pieces, the project brings them together into one workflow.
