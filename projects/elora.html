01 PROJECT OVERVIEW

An e-commerce experience with an operational backend.

ÉLORA was built as more than a storefront. The project combines a customer-facing shopping experience with an internal order management workflow.

Customers can browse products, manage a cart, complete checkout and track orders, while administrative users can manage products, customers and order statuses.
