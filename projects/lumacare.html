LUMACARE / BUSINESS MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

LumaCare

HOME SERVICES
THE PROJECT

More than a website. A working business system.

LumaCare was developed as a complete digital workflow for a home services business. Instead of treating the website as only a customer-facing page, the project connects the customer experience with the operational side of the business.

Customers can request services and manage their bookings, while administrators can manage bookings, customers, leads, schedules, notes, and CRM activity from a centralized workspace.

ROLE Design & Development
STACK HTML / CSS / JavaScript

WHAT I BUILT
01

Online Booking

Customers can select a service, provide booking details, choose requested dates and times, and submit service requests online.

02

Customer Dashboard

Customers can view their account information, bookings, service details, and booking activity from one dashboard.

03

Admin Dashboard

Administrators have a centralized workspace for managing customers, bookings, leads, schedules, and operational information.

04

Booking Management

Booking status, schedules, service information, customer details, and internal notes can be managed within the system.

05

CRM Activity

Customer activity, status changes, scheduling updates, booking events, and internal notes can be tracked through the CRM workflow.

06

Lead Management

Leads can be organized by status, updated with notes, converted into customers, and connected to booking workflows.
