01 PROJECT OVERVIEW

One system for the daily workflow.

LumaCare was built as a practical business management system for a home services company, rather than as a simple landing page.

The system connects the customer-facing experience with internal administrative workflows, making it easier to manage bookings, customers, services and leads.
